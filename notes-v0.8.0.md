**Sound and music.**

- Music, character voices and sound effects play through OnionOS's audio service. Volume follows the handheld's buttons; fine-tune it in Options → Audio.
- The theme's menu and game sound effects are new public-domain sounds made for this port (the originals are licensed to official Panel Attack releases only).
- To fit the Mini Plus's memory, each character keeps up to three versions of each voice clip, and all audio is mono.
- If no sound device is available, the game runs silently instead of failing.

**Updating:** Mixtape offers this as an update and keeps your `data` folder. By hand: replace `App/PanelAttack`, keeping its `data` folder.
