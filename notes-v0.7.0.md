First public build.

- Online versus on panelattack.com over Wi-Fi (lobby, challenges, ranked and casual).
- Endless, Time Attack, Vs Yourself, Puzzles, Training and Challenge modes.
- 60 fps on the Miyoo Mini Plus with a custom software renderer.
- Handheld UI for 640×480: boards side by side with a centre info column, larger text, new menus, lobby and on-screen keyboard.
- Installable and updatable from Mixtape.

Known gaps: no sound yet; local 2-player is not available; the Replays, Controls and puzzle-browser screens still use Panel Attack's original layouts. An FPS counter shows in the bottom-left corner (turn it off in Options → General).

**Install by hand:** delete `App/PanelAttack` if you have an older test build (keep its `data` folder), then copy the `App` folder from the zip to the root of your SD card.
